# Room asset usage

The metadata registry is the source of truth. This map summarizes the selected local derivatives that are actually instantiated; absent entries automatically fall back to CC0 or procedural content.

| Room | Local characters | Local props/environment | Composition role |
|---|---|---|---|
| Grand Library | Bob, Rosie, Raymond; 6 NPCs | Froggy Chair, Mini DIY Workbench, hardcover/paperback books, coffee mug/cup/grinder, desk fan, corkboard, clock, lost book, NookPhone, baskets, bags, seasonal plants | reception, communal study tables, fireplace drinks, shelf/desk storytelling |
| Garden Café | Bob, Rosie, Raymond; 5 NPCs | Froggy Chair, coffee mug, iced tea, basket, oak/big/palm trees, tent, recycle box, seasonal flowers, rocks, weeds | café terrace, pond destination, shaded study, bench grove, dense planted edges |
| Scenic Train | Bob, Rosie, Raymond; 4 NPCs | hardcover books, mug, phone/lost book, tote/handbag, clock, corkboard, desk fan | repeated study bays, luggage stories, carriage fixtures; moving scenery remains procedural |
| Japanese Study Room | Bob, Rosie, Raymond; 5 NPCs | Froggy Chair, Mini DIY Workbench, hardcover/paperback books, phone/lost book, tea/mug, basket, seasonal plants, clock, corkboard | tea-reading entry clusters, low desk study, reading corner, wall accents |

Audited but intentionally unconverted sources include `acnh_nintendo_switch`, `card_stand`, and `rug`. Their unused derivatives were removed; the rug read as an inventory/icon-like grille at gameplay scale, so the rooms use authored runners instead.
